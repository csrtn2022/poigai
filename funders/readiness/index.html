STANDARD DONOR DOSSIER

The seven items every case must earn.

1. Geographic evidence

Site polygon or coordinates, hazard maps, jurisdiction and permissions.

2. Baseline & additionality

Measured conditions, public schemes, inequities and documented unmet need.

3. Independent technical scope

Options appraisal, safeguards, design assumptions and lifecycle alternatives.

4. Financial case

Bill of quantities, competing rates, taxes, contingency, operation and maintenance.

5. Delivery governance

Eligible recipient, implementation partner, maintenance owner, due diligence.

6. Measurable outcomes

Denominators, baseline, scenario targets, verifiers and follow-up dates.

Additional gate: written independent quality assurance and a risk/grievance register. A missing field is marked pending, never silently assumed complete.
