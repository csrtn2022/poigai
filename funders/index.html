Before infrastructure or field trials, a project needs local consent, a baseline and a credible design. You can discuss a separately scoped assessment engagement to establish those foundations.

Compare practical options

03 · COST & REVIEW

Prepare a decision dossier

Develop quantities, cost evidence, a measurement plan and a documented recommendation to proceed, revise or stop.

An assessment can conclude that implementation should not proceed. No assessment price, provider appointment or delivery date is confirmed yet. A written proposal must name the responsible parties, deliverables, fees, recipient, permissions and timeline before acceptance.

BEFORE COMMITMENT

The evidence you
should expect.

A complete funding-review dossier

  • Exact place, community consent and measured baseline.
  • Confirmed unmet need and alternatives considered.
  • Named delivery partner and maintenance owner.
  • Costed design, full lifecycle budget and funding gap.
  • Outcome targets, measurement method and deadlines.
  • Recipient verification, approvals, safeguards and risks.

A reporting plan agreed in writing

The proposed reporting standard separates expenditure, activities delivered and measured outcomes. The agreement should identify the reporting owner, schedule, supporting evidence, delay escalation and how unspent funds or scope changes are handled.

Reports and live progress will be published only when an actual engagement and permissions support them. No project delivery or results are claimed today.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU GIVE

Clear answers. No pressure.

Can I donate now?

This website does not accept donations. You can read the concept briefs, discuss an assessment scope or explore a partnership. A funding request should follow verified readiness and an appropriate recipient route.

Can I support a specific place or cause?

Yes, you can express a preference for water, heat or farm resilience and a location. This records an enquiry, not an earmarked gift or a promise that a proposed project will proceed.

Will I receive a tax deduction?

No tax benefit is promised by POIGAI. Eligibility depends on the actual recipient, the donor, the jurisdiction and the transaction. Ask for current evidence and independent advice before committing.

Are you partnered with the organisations cited?

Research citations are evidence sources. They do not mean endorsement, accreditation or a partnership with POIGAI. No implementation or international giving partner is currently appointed in this portfolio.

What if the project changes or is not feasible?

The assessment should recommend proceeding, revising or stopping based on evidence. Any future funding agreement should define approvals for changes, unspent funds, refunds where applicable and reporting duties before funds move.

Must I create an account or share financial details?

No. Briefs are public and an enquiry needs only basic contact details, a role and consent. Do not send identity documents, card details or sensitive beneficiary information through the enquiry form.
