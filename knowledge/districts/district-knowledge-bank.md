# POIGAI — District Climate Problems & Responses

Edition 1.1 · 2026-10-15

144 districts across five southern states.

District names checked against government directories. Contexts, questions, problem selections and proposed actions are POIGAI planning synthesis using regional briefs and district source routes, not 144 completed district risk assessments. No severity ranking or confirmed local unmet need is claimed. Consult the cited district plan/maps and obtain local measurements before project selection.

All three starting problems are unranked planning suggestions. No confirmed local unmet need, partner appointment, budget or delivery date is implied.

## Ariyalur — Tamil Nadu

inland villages and cultivated plains

**Local question:** Which village water sources fail before the next dependable rains?

**Status:** Planning screen — local verification pending

### Dry-season water reliability

Problem to check: Check whether drinking-water sources, wells and storage remain dependable through dry months.

Response to assess: Prepare a seasonal water budget, test source quality and compare leak repair, demand reduction and hydrogeologically suitable recharge.

Baseline: Monthly supply days, well levels, abstraction and water-quality tests.

Measures: Reliable service days and seasonal extraction, with water quality maintained.

Roles to appoint: Local body, water utility, hydrogeologist and participating water users.

Evidence guide: https://www.poigai.com/knowledge/#K02

### Rainfall-sensitive crops and farm income

Problem to check: Check crop-stage dry spells, excess-rain damage and income sensitivity for locally grown crops.

Response to assess: Co-design a small comparable farmer trial with KVK support: crop timing, locally suited varieties, soil cover and water budgeting.

Baseline: Crop-wise multi-season yield, rainfall, labour, input costs and farm water use.

Measures: Yield stability and net return including household labour, compared across seasons.

Roles to appoint: Farmer group or FPO, KVK agronomist and independent evaluator.

Evidence guide: https://www.poigai.com/knowledge/#K11

### Heat in homes, workplaces and institutions

Problem to check: Check occupied indoor heat and outdoor work exposure, including humidity and access to shade.

Response to assess: Audit a school, health centre or worksite; compare roof treatment, shading, ventilation and professionally designed heat-response arrangements.

Baseline: Occupied-hour indoor/outdoor temperature, humidity, occupancy and energy use.

Measures: Hours above an agreed comfort threshold; protected work periods; weather-adjusted electricity.

Roles to appoint: Building owner, facilities operator, occupational-health team and qualified designer.

Evidence guide: https://www.poigai.com/knowledge/#K06

Evidence routes: https://lokbhavan.tn.gov.in/districts-of-tamil-nadu/

## Chengalpattu — Tamil Nadu

coastal settlements, tanks and the urban fringe

**Local question:** Which tank-linked settlements lose drainage or safe access during heavy rain?

**Status:** Planning screen — local verification pending

### Urban waterlogging and polluted water bodies

Problem to check: Check storm-drain connectivity, sewage inflows and low-lying homes or public buildings.

Response to assess: Trace catchments and inlets, separate pollution control from flood management, and compare utility repairs with retention and wetland options.

Baseline: Drain connections, inlet flows and pollution loads; inundation records and treatment uptime.

Measures: Compliant treated flows; comparable-storm waterlogging duration; maintenance completed.

Roles to appoint: Municipal body, sewer/drainage utility, asset custodian and community representatives.

Evidence guide: https://www.poigai.com/knowledge/#K03

### Coastal storms, erosion and fishing access

Problem to check: Check exposed shore settlements, boat access, shelter routes and seasonal shoreline change.

Response to assess: Assess sediment and coastal hazards before selecting setbacks, dunes, habitat recovery or engineered protection; protect fishing access and neighbouring shores.

Baseline: Seasonal beach profiles, shoreline history, exposure and evacuation travel time.

Measures: Exposure avoided; livelihood access retained; shoreline response monitored.

Roles to appoint: Coastal authority, district administration, fisheries teams and fishing communities.

Problem to check: Check seasonal saltwater intrusion or saline sources before treating a freshwater shortage as a quantity-only problem.

Response to assess: Test conductivity and relevant water-quality parameters through wet and dry periods; assess source protection and demand management with water specialists.

Baseline: Seasonal conductivity, water-quality tests, source availability and abstraction.

Measures: Water meeting the intended-use standard and reliable freshwater service days.

Roles to appoint: Water utility, accredited laboratory, hydrogeologist and local users.
