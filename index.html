YOUR PRIVACY & EXPECTATIONS

A conversation.
A considered decision.

POIGAI provides climate and CSR project advisory support. An enquiry creates no payment obligation, grant application or funding commitment. A response deadline is not yet published.

How enquiry data is used

We use your name, email, role and any optional information to assess and respond to your enquiry. Draft preparation stays in this page’s memory until you choose to email it. When online submission is available, Cloudflare stores the enquiry with consent time and a reference. Enquiries are not published or used to opt you into marketing.

Do not send identity documents, passwords or sensitive beneficiary information. For access, correction, deletion or a privacy concern, email contact@poigai.com. A fixed retention period and named privacy owner are pending publication; ask about these before sharing more than basic enquiry details.
