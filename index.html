CLIMATE SCIENCE MEETS PROJECT FINANCE — THE EVIDENCE STANDARD

Credible climate action starts with a defensible baseline.

A proposal is more convincing when its environmental problem, intervention logic, assumptions and measurement plan can be examined. Our role is to help structure those questions before claims are made.

01 / DIAGNOSE

Context & baseline

Define local environmental pressures, affected communities, existing conditions and material data gaps.

02 / DESIGN

Intervention logic

Link proposed activities to achievable outcomes, responsibilities, budgets and known limitations.

03 / MEASURE

Evidence & learning

Select suitable indicators, monitoring intervals and reporting practices without promising unverified results.

Scientific framing is not a substitute for statutory approvals, expert site assessments or independent validation.
