FIVE SHARED GATES

Evidence protects every partnership.

1 · Validated need

Exact location, consent, unmet gap and seasonal baseline.

2 · Qualified entities

Recipient/implementer identity, capacity and applicable statutory eligibility.

3 · Credible investment case

Costed scope, procurement, safeguards, maintenance and outcome targets.

4 · Written agreement

Human-approved funding, roles, milestones and grievance controls.

5 · Independent outcomes

Proof of delivery, expenditure checks and follow-up against baseline.

The timelines on this page are planning illustrations, not contractual turnaround commitments. POIGAI currently does not collect donor funds online. Verification and matching software is operational, but end-to-end transaction and third-party audit sign-off remain gated.
