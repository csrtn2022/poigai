POIGAI Website — Version 1 starter

Files: index.html, styles.css, script.js, README.txt.

This is a responsive single-page public website prototype for POIGAI CSR & Impact Partners. It positions POIGAI as a connector and project-development/advisory partner for NGOs, corporate CSR teams and technical/implementation partners.

Before launch:
1. Confirm the legal entity that operates the website and add accurate details.
2. Confirm all described services reflect what POIGAI actually provides.
3. Configure an official contact email or secure form service. The current form does not transmit/store submissions; it prepares an email draft.
4. Add a privacy notice and terms appropriate to data collected.
5. Add verified contact information and social links.
6. Do not imply guaranteed CSR funding or corporate/government endorsement.
7. Upload the files to a hosting service and connect poigai.com and www.poigai.com in the domain DNS settings.

Preview: open index.html in a modern browser. Google Fonts need internet; fallback fonts are included.
