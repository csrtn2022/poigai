WHO WE ARE

Climate Action Partners.
Preparation before promises.

POIGAI’s proposed role is to help NGOs, communities, funders and technical specialists turn environmental needs into better-prepared projects. The initial focus is water reliability, institutional heat and dryland farm resilience in South India.

Advisory work may include readiness reviews, project design, partnership development and measurement planning under an agreed scope. Funders make their own decisions; delivery and maintenance roles require named, accepted responsibilities.

FUNDING & FEES

A clear separation
of responsibilities.

Project funds

No donation recipient or receiving account is verified or published for these concepts. A future funding arrangement must identify the eligible recipient, permitted account, purpose, approval process and reporting duties before funds move.

No Indian intermediary re-granting or pass-through route is offered for foreign contributions. Overseas interest requires donor/source classification and current recipient eligibility review.

Advisory fees

Any POIGAI paid service needs a separate written scope, legal contracting identity, deliverables, fee and payment terms. An advisory fee must not be described as a charitable donation or a guaranteed route to funding.

The proposed disclosure standard is to show preparation, implementation, monitoring, administration and maintenance costs separately. No “100% to the cause”, match funding or tax-deductibility claim is made.

Applicable FCRA, CSR and tax requirements need case-specific verification. Official reference: Ministry of Home Affairs FCRA portal. International giving context: CAF America’s India giving guidance (historical guidance; verify current requirements). Cited organisations are not claimed as POIGAI partners.

THE PROJECT STANDARD

Seven fields.
One readiness decision.

Every demonstrator must have an exact location, measured baseline, confirmed unmet need, appointed implementation partner, accepted maintenance owner, costed design and measurable outcome with a target and deadline.

All seven require evidence and review, with critical permissions and technical issues resolved. Passing this gate means a dossier is ready for funding review; it does not guarantee funding or results.

PRIVACY, SAFEGUARDS & CONCERNS

Respect for people.
Space for questions.

Community dignity and evidence

The proposed project standard requires community participation, site access permissions and appropriate safeguards before field activity. Future stories and photographs need appropriate consent; illustrative images must stay labelled. No beneficiary testimonial or completed-project photograph is presented as proof of delivery.

Raise a concern

Email contact@poigai.com with a brief description. This is the current contact route. A dedicated complaints owner, handling policy and response timeline are pending publication. Do not include sensitive personal records in an initial email.

Your enquiry

No account is needed. The enquiry asks for basic contact information and consent; further context is optional. Drafts stay in page memory until you choose to email them. Online storage is used only when the service explicitly reports availability and confirms a reference.

Enquiries do not subscribe you to marketing. Access, correction and deletion requests go to contact@poigai.com. A fixed retention period and named privacy owner are pending publication.
