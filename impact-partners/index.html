EXPLORE · VALIDATE · BUILD · DELIVER

Your complete implementation journey

Timelines are indicative elapsed planning windows, not guaranteed review SLAs, appointments or project launch dates. The next stage begins only when its evidence gate is satisfied.

01 · DISCOVER

Explore the problems and opportunities

Days 1–7 · indicative

Choose your district and the climate issue your organisation understands best. Review source quality, screening categories and existing public schemes.

  • District opportunities and Puducherry regions
  • Research evidence bank
Stage output: selected issue, locality and initial opportunity statement.
02 · EXPRESS INTEREST

Introduce your organisation

Week 1–2 · indicative

Register your own stakeholder account with verified email. Provide organisation identity, geographic experience, community contacts and interest through the consent-based enquiry channel.

  • Register or sign in
  • Discuss a local project need
Stage output: verified email and enquiry reference; organisation approval remains a separate review.
03 · QUALIFY THE NEED

Verify the site and unmet need

Weeks 2–6 · indicative

Document the exact village, ward, asset or catchment; communities affected; baseline and consent; permissions; local hazards; and gaps not covered by existing schemes.

  • Field indicators and impact registers
  • Safeguard mapping, photo evidence and official plan references
Gate: identifiable site + substantiated unmet need + legitimate counterpart.
04 · DEVELOP A PROJECT

Build a costed implementation dossier

Months 2–4 · indicative

Work with qualified technical contributors to compare solutions and prepare a design, bill of quantities, operating cost, project schedule, community safeguards and measurable targets.

  • Eight solution strategies
  • Donor dossier and readiness checklist
Gate: costed and independently reviewed design, delivery and maintenance roles.
05 · DUE DILIGENCE & MATCHING

Become eligible for donor consideration

Months 3–6+ · depends on evidence

Submit organisational legal credentials, governance and financial history, relevant statutory registrations, safeguarding procedures, delivery track record and authorised bank/recipient arrangements. POIGAI and prospective funders assess fit separately.

  • CSR/foreign-contribution eligibility requires case-specific legal review.
  • No funding commitment follows automatically from registration or screening.
Gate: eligibility validated, suitable funding route and written agreement.
06 · IMPLEMENT & REPORT

Execute milestone-based delivery

Typically months 6–24 · scope-dependent

When approved and contracted, mobilise partners, procure transparently, supervise site work, manage grievances and report milestones, invoices, changes and results.

  • Community participation, safety and environmental safeguards
  • Independent inspections and expenditure reconciliation
Gate: commissioning evidence and agreed handover.
07 · MAINTAIN & PROVE IMPACT

Show what changed and who maintains it

6, 12 and where relevant 36 months after delivery

Re-measure against the original baseline across comparable seasons. Report unique people, livestock, surviving native trees, waterbody condition, service reliability, farm outcomes and maintenance performance only where relevant.

  • Measurement and reporting methodology
  • Publish outcomes, uncertainty, lessons and ongoing stewardship arrangements
Stage output: independently reviewable impact report and maintenance evidence.

READINESS YOU CAN CONTROL

Prepare these before requesting investment review.

Organisation identity

Registration, key officers, jurisdiction, authorised contact and governance.

Local need

Evidence of climate exposure, affected households and gaps in current programmes.

Execution ability

Staffing, relevant past work, technical partners, procurement and safeguarding capacity.

Budget

Detailed quantities, local rates, contingency and lifecycle maintenance costs.

Accountability

Permissions, fund recipient eligibility, financial controls, grievance process and maintenance owner.

Impact

Baseline, time-bound targets, repeatable indicators, consent and verification methods.

Current platform limitation: POIGAI supports account registration, verification and enquiry capture, but a self-service project submission/review/status tracking system has not yet been implemented. The pathway describes the supported preparation and coordination process, not a functioning automated approval workflow.
