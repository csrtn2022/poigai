# POIGAI Stakeholder Journey Architecture — Decision v1.0
Date: 2026-10-15
Status: Approved design requirement; implementation pending.

## Principle
Every stakeholder-facing journey on poigai.com must show a clear, numbered stage sequence, inspired by the user's approved multi-step setup reference. A stakeholder should always understand:
- Where the journey began and what has been completed.
- The current stage and its required action.
- Which stages remain, and what happens next.
- A meaningful indication of overall progress without inventing completion or duration estimates.

## Experience requirements
1. Display a persistent, accessible stepper on desktop and mobile with numbered stages, current-stage emphasis, completed-stage confirmation and upcoming stages.
2. Provide a concise stage title, purpose, required inputs, expected outputs, and a clear next action.
3. Permit safe return to earlier completed stages without losing consented, saved progress where technically supported.
4. Show blockers, missing documents, review status and decision ownership honestly.
5. Use POIGAI's approved premium dark forest-green design; high contrast, keyboard accessibility, mobile readability and reduced motion.
6. Avoid fake percentages, deadlines, grants, approvals or automatic transitions. Status must be evidence-based.
7. Keep personal and organisational confidential data out of public URLs, logs and unauthenticated pages.

## Stakeholder journeys (proposed outlines; validate with actual services before publishing)
### NGOs/community organisations
01 Explore fit -> 02 Introduce organisation -> 03 Readiness checklist -> 04 Project concept -> 05 Documentation review -> 06 Partnership discussion -> 07 Decision and next steps.
### Corporate CSR teams
01 Define CSR priorities -> 02 Identify community themes -> 03 Evaluate project concepts -> 04 Due diligence -> 05 Partnership design -> 06 Approval decision -> 07 Monitoring and learning.
### Technical/implementation partners
01 Identify capabilities -> 02 Register interest -> 03 Eligibility and evidence -> 04 Scope alignment -> 05 Terms and responsibilities -> 06 Participation decision -> 07 Delivery and reporting.
### Project proponents/community representatives
01 Express need -> 02 Describe context -> 03 Validate evidence -> 04 Co-design outcomes -> 05 Feasibility and consent -> 06 Partner assessment -> 07 Feedback and next steps.

## Incremental delivery
Phase A: Public explanation of journeys and honest progress steps, without pretending online submission exists.
Phase B: Secure intake forms and consent, with verified email delivery.
Phase C: Authenticated case tracking with actual saved status and reviewer actions.
Phase D: Evidence-based reporting and stakeholder dashboards only after operational validation.

## Acceptance criteria
All stakeholder paths visibly identify start/current/remaining stages; real stage statuses are never fabricated; navigation works on mobile; consent and privacy are explicit; public pages never expose internal case data.
