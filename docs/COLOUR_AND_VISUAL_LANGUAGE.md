# POIGAI colour and visual language

Charcoal #101815 is the page foundation, forest #14231C / #193126 separates surfaces, emerald #159A70 identifies primary actions with dark text, ivory #F5F1E8 carries primary text, muted #B7C4BB carries supporting text, mint #64D6AC identifies links and emphasis, and gold #C8AC70 is reserved for short labels and accents.

Essential text pairings have calculated WCAG contrast ratios above 4.5:1: ivory/charcoal 16.01, muted/raised-forest 7.71, dark/emerald 5.05, mint/forest 9.14, and gold/forest 7.45. These checks cover the specified token combinations, not a full accessibility audit.

Four original SVG illustrations support water reliability, institutional heat protection, dryland agriculture and schematic regional orientation. They are not site photographs, engineering designs, survey maps or evidence of completed projects. The schematic explicitly says not to scale; exact project sites remain pending.

Use permission-cleared, dated and location-captioned photographs when real sites are confirmed. Do not present generic photography or generated imagery as POIGAI delivery evidence. The visual preview in this folder shows the artwork and palette, not a browser screenshot.

The shared theme applies to homepage, projects and accounts. Stakeholder logic, authentication activation and enquiry capture settings are unchanged. This review branch requires publication approval before becoming live.
