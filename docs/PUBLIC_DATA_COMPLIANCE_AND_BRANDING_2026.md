# POIGAI — Public Data, Legal Compliance and Brand Release Gates
Edition 2026-10-16 · Operational draft for review by qualified Indian counsel · Not a legal opinion

## Operating identity and positioning
POIGAI: **Climate Action & Impact Stewardship**. Descriptive role: *connecting evidence-led climate needs, qualified Impact Partners and responsible Funders*. Do not claim to be a government agency, approved registry, certified financial intermediary, funding recipient or charitable trust unless relevant verified entity documentation establishes that status. On every page distinguish (1) science and public data, (2) POIGAI's own screening synthesis, (3) verified site baselines, (4) proposed projects, (5) funded projects, and (6) outcomes independently measured.

## Public directory: permitted and blocked
- Currently permitted: curated district climate screening data, cited government and scientific sources, public institutional descriptions with attribution, POIGAI project concepts, non-personal outcome/impact categories and CSV extracts of the same.
- Not permitted by default: copying NGO Darpan or ngosindia listings wholesale, scraping behind terms or technical barriers, republishing personal mobile numbers, emails or identifiable beneficiary records, presenting scraped entries as verified POIGAI partners, unauthorised use of NITI Aayog logos or the National Emblem, publishing bank/registration documents uploaded privately, releasing funder contact lists without rights or consent.
- A public NGO/funder record requires: named organisational identity; record origin and permission/licence to republish; verification status and verification date; authorised public description and contact channel; source link; correction/removal route; no endorsement implication; expiry/review policy.
- Registry presence is not certification. NGO Darpan explicitly says its ID does not confer endorsement, affiliation or recognition by NITI Aayog.

## Data protection and security
Use privacy-by-design: transparent notice, specific purpose, data minimisation, consent and records of consent, retention schedule, correction/withdrawal request channel, access restrictions and audit log. No beneficiary microdata in public exports. Avoid mixing personal accounts with public organisation profiles. Review the staged commencement, 2025 rules and applicability of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 with qualified counsel before collecting sensitive project/personnel data or expanding uploads. Separate verified role from legal eligibility.

## CSR and corporate funds
Before stating a partner is eligible for Indian corporate CSR delivery, verify specific entity type and the current MCA CSR framework, registrations and CSR-1 requirements where applicable. Corporate and NGO responsibilities, permitted CSR activities, agreements and monitoring should be assessed case by case. POIGAI must not claim that any enquiry constitutes a CSR award or a fundraising commitment.

## International donors and FCRA
Never promise a foreign-source transfer until both the recipient and specific funding arrangement have had an FCRA eligibility/prior-permission review where applicable, alongside banking, AML/KYC, tax, recipient entity and contractual checks. POIGAI does not currently process donor payments through the public site.

## Other release reviews
1. Legal and entity disclosure, privacy notice, terms of use, grievance/contact policy, cookies/analytics, intellectual-property rights and accessibility.
2. Non-misleading claims and source attribution; avoid implying government endorsement or partnership.
3. Fundraising agreements, fee structure, related-party disclosures and conflicts of interest.
4. Records of audit, data quality, data corrections, changes in district boundaries, verification and expiry.
5. Vulnerability assessment, privileged admin MFA, session revocation, encrypted uploads, antivirus/content validation, least privilege and access logs before implementing partner workspaces.

## Next implementation gates
