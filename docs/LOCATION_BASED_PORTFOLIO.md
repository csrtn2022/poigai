Approved user requirement, 15 October 2026: stakeholders must be able to see what problem POIGAI addresses, where, with timeline and resources. Seven readiness fields remain mandatory before funding review.

Public route: /projects/. Homepage navigation, hero and a three-project summary link to it. Filters cover project family and stakeholder role. Roles cover communities/NGOs, corporates/funders, technical partners and asset/maintenance owners. Filters do not create accounts, submit applications or claim partnerships.

Initial proposals: D01 community water reliability in Ramanathapuram; D02 heat-resilient institutions in Tiruchirappalli; D03 dryland farm resilience in the historical Anantapur region. Exact sites, field baselines, unmet needs, delivery partners, maintenance owners, costed designs and numerical outcome targets are not verified. No project is presented as ready for funding review, funded or in implementation.

Each card includes the regional evidence link, proposed action, next action, seven readiness fields, eight numbered stages and required human/technical/financial/maintenance resources. Timelines are POIGAI provisional planning allowances, not evidence-based duration commitments: preparation 6–12 weeks after access and funded mandate; conditional water works 3–6 months; heat retrofit 2–6 weeks with comparable hot-weather measurement; agriculture follows agreed crop seasons with a proposed two-season evaluation window subject to agronomic review. Start dates and completion dates are unconfirmed. Publish committed dates only after approval and resource confirmation.
